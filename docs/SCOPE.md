# Current platform scope

Mosaic is a source-reviewed learning platform with Lanternbound and eight additional game templates. The [README](../README.md) lists implemented features; [deployment](DEPLOYMENT.md) records which host is actually live.

## Learning and content

An author imports bounded source material, checks extraction and marks the exact version reviewed. Selected passages ground a Muse Wonder request. The server validates the schema, references and template rules, attaches exact quotations and stores a draft. An author checks meaning, solutions and suitability before publication or assignment. Unsupported evidence is a reason to revise the material or decline generation.

A programme can connect a game, source reading, Explore lesson, question cycle, podcast and cartoon. Personalisation considers goals, context/exams, starting level, interests, difficulties, accessibility and recorded responses. Suggestions explain their basis and can be overridden. Repeated questions and game success do not establish unaided mastery. Open reasoning remains for human review.

The tutor supports text and voice: explicitly send a recording for transcription, edit the question, then ask Muse. Spoken replies use private saved audio. Guidance remains labelled unreviewed. Managed learners need an organiser's tutor permission, which does not grant authoring rights.

## Games and reusable art

Contracts: `underlight-v1`, `adventure-v1`, `draft-v1`, `town-v1`, `economy-v1`, `novel-v1`, `quiz-v1`, `sports-v1` and `merge-v1`. Rules, geometry, collisions and asset catalogues are authored. Content changes story, examples, questions and bounded scenario choices; it cannot supply executable rules or arbitrary URLs.

Lanternbound has eight connected regions, eighteen interiors, residents, shops, side requests, party equipment/abilities and the five-room Underlight chapter. These are connected scenes, not a streamed open world. Illustrated games and economic simulations have distinct loops and endings. Economic quantities are fictional teaching assumptions, not forecasts or complete historical models.

Polyfork defines the RPG asset direction. New Meshy humanoids use 7.1 with reviewed T-pose inputs, preserved skeletons and motion/deformation checks. Reusable 2D scenes, objects, character sheets and cartoon staging provide variety within curated art. See the [game and art references](README.md).

## Identity, data and operations

NoCodeBackend verifies identity and stores versioned learner preferences/progress. Membership, age-band onboarding, ownership and assignments are checked separately. Public hosting does not make private learning records public or grant new accounts authoring access.

The platform includes explicit sharing/withdrawal, exact-version assignments, request/cost budgets, saved media, exports, resumable deletion, retention and recovery tooling. Hosting migration must preserve existing IDs, hashes and ownership. Identity removal requires the operator step after data cleanup.

## What is not established

- Any-subject/every-language accuracy, learning efficacy or a validated mastery/ability score.
- Accessibility conformance from DOM checks or desktop resizing. Physical mobile, microphone and representative screen-reader sessions need observed evidence.
- Institution-scale capacity, a disaster-recovery SLA or zero data loss across separate services.
- High-stakes auto-grading, proctoring, attention/emotion analytics, admissions decisions, multiplayer or SIS integration.
- Unrestricted generation of new game engines, whole open worlds, arbitrary assets or video footage.

Pre-migration acceptance passed 632 tests and the build, plus real provider journeys and bounded browser checks. Independent educator/learner evaluation, a completed independent code audit and larger production-like load testing remain necessary. Historical reports retain their actual scope and limits.
